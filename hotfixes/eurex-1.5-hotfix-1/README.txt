EUR 1.5 Fix Pack (EUREX 1.5 only)

Install: extract into your mods folder, overwrite, restart the game.

Fixes:
- Custom city models (Bree, Amon Hen, etc.) going generic in the second campaign of a session
- Amon Hen campaign model restored; Hobbiton and Bree custom battle maps keyed to the right tiles
- Unit-upgrade / bodyguard crest disappearing for the rest of the session
- Reclaiming Moria quest (Durin's Folk, Mirkwood route): "Restricted area" on the arrow target. New campaign needed.
- Green outlines under monster area attacks (debug switch left on in 1.5)
- Custom settlement battle maps (Tharbad, Weathertop, Hobbiton, Bree and the rest) never loading; the picker was never switched on

Install note: EUREX1.5M2EX.7z is the engine package. Extract it into the game root
(Medieval II Total War\), never into the mod folder. A stray data\descr_caps_ex.txt inside
the mod folder stops every unit model from loading.

EUR 1.5.2 Fix Pack (EUREX 1.5 only)

Install: extract into your mods folder, overwrite, restart the game.
Replaces the 1.5.1 pack; extract it over the top.

Fixes:
- Custom city models (Bree, Amon Hen, etc.) going generic in the second campaign of a session
- Amon Hen campaign model restored, also in older saves
- Custom settlement battle maps (Tharbad, Weathertop, Hobbiton and the rest) never loading; the map picker was never switched on
- Hobbiton battle map keyed to its new tile; Bree's own map added (the game may still pick the generic one for Bree)
- Unit-upgrade / bodyguard crest disappearing for the rest of the session
- Reclaiming Moria quest (Durin's Folk, Mirkwood route): "Restricted area" on the arrow target. Start a new campaign to be sure.
- Green outlines under monster area attacks (debug switch left on in 1.5)
- Map props (stone trolls, Stone of Erech...) no longer pile up after every battle

Install note: EUREX1.5M2EX.7z is the engine package. Extract it into the game root
(Medieval II Total War\), never into the mod folder. A stray data\descr_caps_ex.txt inside
the mod folder stops every unit model from loading.

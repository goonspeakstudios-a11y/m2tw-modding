Aragorn Journey Fix (Divide and Conquer EUR on M2EX)

Install: extract this zip into your Medieval II Total War\mods folder and say Yes to overwrite. Restart the game. Works on existing saves.

Fixes: Aragorn's quest (Bree, Cameth Brin, Rivendell, Weathertop, Edoras, Minas Tirith) never advancing, because M2EX does not run the region checks the quest relies on.

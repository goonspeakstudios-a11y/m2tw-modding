DIVIDE AND CONQUER EUR - FIX PACK for EUREX 1.4.2 (M2EX)

INSTALL
Extract into your Medieval II Total War\mods folder. Say yes to overwrite.
Restart the game. Works on existing saves.

WHAT IT FIXES
- Cities with a unique look (Minas Tirith and 46 others) no longer turn into
  generic towns on the campaign map when "Automatic culture conversion" is on.
- Campaign-map city models now load every time you load a save, not only the
  first time after starting the game.
- The mod's special battle maps for certain towns (Tharbad, Weathertop,
  Hobbiton and others) are now used. The code that picks them was never
  switched on.
- Hobbiton's battle map pointed at the wrong spot. Bree now has its own.
- Amon Hen gets its own campaign-map model.
- Barrow-wights were missing from the unit voice list.
- Aragorn's quest no longer gets stuck after the first "go to Bree" message.

ARAGORN'S QUEST
End Aragorn's turn in each place. The message comes the next turn.
  1. Bree-land (or Combe, Archet, Trestlebridge), after the turn 5 message
  2. Rhudaur Uplands (Cameth Brin)
  3. Bree-land again
  4. Hidden Valley (Imladris), 2 turns
